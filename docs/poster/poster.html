What is Henna
(Mehndi)?

Henna is a plant-based temporary body art made from henna powder and essential oils. It is a beautiful way to celebrate weddings, birthdays, parties, baby showers, festivals and all of life's special moments.

Every design is drawn by hand, made for you, with 100% natural organic henna.

Caring for your henna

Henna Aftercare

  • Keep the henna paste on for at least 2 to 3 hours.
  • The longer you keep it on, the darker the stain may become.
  • Gently scrape off the dried paste; do not wash it off.
  • Avoid water for at least 6 to 8 hours.
  • The stain looks lighter at first and deepens over the next 24 to 48 hours.
